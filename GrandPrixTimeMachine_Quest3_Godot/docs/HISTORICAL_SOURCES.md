# Fuji 1976 editorial verification ledger

This is an **interactive illustration**, not a faithful historic race timing feed.
The six *presentation seconds* (0, 14, 30, 58, 83, 104) are the author's
screenplay positions. They are **not race timestamps or lap assignments**.

## Illustrative content that must not be treated as fact

- Circuit shape, width, and corner geometry are placeholders; this is **not**
  the period Fuji layout, satellite data, or survey data.
- Four toy-style cars have generic self-made geometry and approximate liveries,
  not authentic models. Every race position is a synthetic animation.
- Motion cycles, rain particle density, graphics, sound, scale and placement are
  aesthetic decisions, not 1976 camera footage or measurements.
- Driver biographies and actual photographs are not embedded. No third-party
  video, brand logos or audio was copied into this prototype.

## Future research acceptance

Before labeling a reconstructed circuit accurate, require a period-source map
of the 1976 Fuji configuration, a reproducible method for mapping geometry into
3D, and separate track-level validation. Current v0.4 fails that acceptance
criterion by design, so its labels must continue to say `ILLUSTRATIVE`.
